HOW TO PLAY

Same size → MERGEBump things your size. Each merge fills your grow meter. Full meter = you grow into something bigger.
Bigger → BONKRed glow = bigger than you. It knocks you down a size. Smaller vehicles you can wreck for cash. People always hop out of the way.
☎ Phones → MISSIONSBump a ringing phone. Follow the gold arrow. Missions pay big.
Drift, jump, close callsDrift then let go for a boost. Hit yellow ramps at speed to fly over traffic. Whizz past big stuff for a close-call bonus. Chain it all for a combo.
★ Heat → COPSBig forms and wrecks raise heat. Wrecking a police vehicle: a whole star. At 2 stars police hunt you, always one size bigger. As a walker, cops BUST you and the run ends.
👀 Lose the copsCops only chase what they see. Break line of sight behind buildings: stars flash, they search, then give up. A car wash clears 2 stars at once.
👑 Careers → LEGENDYour color earns reputation for its career: Office Worker, Police, Mobster or Rescue. Climb the ranks across runs. Top rank = Legend. Legend in all four = you own the city.
RecipesMix colors in one meter for a secret recipe form.
